MFR-303 - free acid bass synthesizer by Music For Robots
https://musicforrobots.com
Source code: https://github.com/music-for-robots/MFR-303

INSTALLING (zip version)
  Copy the MFR-303.vst3 folder into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (in FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins
  > Find plugins). MFR-303.exe is a standalone version that runs without
  a DAW.

PLAYING IT
  Sequencer OFF (MIDI mode): play it from your piano roll or keyboard.
    Notes with velocity 100 or higher are accented, and overlapping
    notes slide.
  Sequencer ON: the 16-step pattern plays when your DAW's transport is
    running. Hold a MIDI key to transpose the pattern (middle C = no
    transpose).

  Knobs: double-click or Alt-click resets to the default.
         Ctrl- or Shift-drag gives fine control.
         Click the value under a knob to type in a number.
  Grid:  drag a NOTE box up/down or use the scroll wheel. Right-click a
         NOTE box to toggle the gate. Drag across a row of buttons to
         paint them.
